Dental Radiograph Detection with Modified Backbone

Prior work, published and presented at the International Conference on Sustainable Information Engineering and Technology (SIET), utilized a YOLOv11 pipeline trained over 500 epochs to achieve 91% accuracy and a 10.3 ms inference time in dental radiograph analysis. To optimize this further, this study introduces a modified YOLOv9 architecture incorporating ShuffleNetV2 blocks, which demonstrates rapid convergence by reaching 75% accuracy at only 50 epochs.
